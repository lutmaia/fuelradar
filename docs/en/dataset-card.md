# Dataset card

Generated by `scripts/dataset_card.py` from the database; no number is typed by hand. Details in `reports/dataset/ficha.json`; source files with SHA-256 in `reports/dataset/fontes.csv`.

## Origin

ANP's fuel price survey (Levantamento de Preços de Combustíveis, LPC), weekly files by retail station, plus ANP's historical releases for the earlier period. External sources: US dollar rate (Central Bank of Brazil, SGS 1) and ANP producer and distributor prices.

## Coverage

State of São Paulo, from 2022-11-13 to 2026-10-04: 174 weeks with data out of 204 in the range, 108 municipalities and 4,009 stations. 801,810 current observations.

Weeks without data, never filled: 19 weeks with no file on ANP's page (2022-11-27; 2022-12-25; 2023-01-08; 2023-04-02; 2023-04-16 – 2023-04-30; 2023-12-31; 2024-03-03; 2024-04-14; 2024-05-12; 2024-12-22; 2025-10-19 – 2025-11-30) and 11 weeks whose ANP file is defective, left out by a recorded decision in `config/excluded_sources.json`, each with its reason: spreadsheets the reader cannot open or that hold summaries instead of stations (2022-12-04 – 2022-12-18; 2023-01-01; 2023-02-26 – 2023-03-26; 2023-04-09; 2025-06-29).

## Sample size

In each municipality and week, the median is 8 surveyed stations (10% of cases with 3 or fewer, 10% with 15 or more). 8,279 of the 85,249 municipality-week cells (9.7%) have fewer than 3 stations and are treated as weak series.

## Quality

8,653 observations (1.1%) carry a warning (extreme price for the fuel: 8,653); they stay in the database and in the medians. 114,204 rows are not current because another ANP file holds the same data (revision or precedence between sources, ADR 0002). Quarantined (left out): 8 (different prices for the same key).

## Biases and limitations

- ANP surveys a **sample** of stations, not all of them; small municipalities have few stations and irregular series.
- The sample changes from week to week; a municipality's median can move because the stations changed, not only the prices.
- Retail sale price; purchase price is rare and not used.
- Publication gaps stay as gaps: nothing is interpolated.
- The brand is the one declared in each week's file; brand changes over time are not reconstructed.

## Privacy and licenses

- ANP files include the station's tax id (CNPJ), legal name and address. The local database keeps them; the public demo package and the reports do **not** include tax id or legal name.
- ANP data reproduced for non-commercial purposes, with citation and link, under the gov.br portal terms of use. The dollar series (ODbL) is in the database but not in the public package.
- Raw files are not in the repository; `fontes.csv` lists each one's SHA-256 and the URL of those downloaded by the project (historical and aggregate files obtained by hand in milestone 1 have no recorded URL).
